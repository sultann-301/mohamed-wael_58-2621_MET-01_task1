DEW26 lab task 1

I started with 39 signup rows and found inconsistent faculty, city, and club labels, mixed fee-payment representations, inconsistent name capitalization and spacing, email formatting differences, two timestamp formats, and duplicate signups. First, I mapped faculty, city, and club aliases to canonical values and converted fee-payment values to True/False. Next, I standardized name spacing and capitalization, checked that each student ID had one name spelling, and trimmed and lowercased emails. I then converted signup timestamps to datetime, interpreting slash dates as day/month/year because their first component exceeds 12, while preserving the time. After standardization, I displayed exact duplicates and removed 3 extra copies, leaving 36 rows. I then inspected repeated student–club pairs and removed 4 older submissions, leaving 32 rows. Keeping the latest submission preserves the student's most recent recorded signup details rather than potentially outdated information from the first. I fixed club spellings before removing duplicates because pandas compares the stored labels: Robotics and robotics look different until standardized. Without fixing club spellings first, removing 3 exact copies leaves 36 rows, then removing only 2 detected repeated signups leaves 34. After standardizing clubs, pandas detects 4 repeated signups instead, leaving 32 rows. Therefore, skipping club standardization would miss 2 duplicate signups. I identified people by student ID, not name, so the two students named Mohamed Adel (55-2992 and 61-4844) remained separate. Finally, I asserted student–club uniqueness and exported the 32 cleaned rows to club_signups_clean.csv without the pandas index.
